When channels tell different stories

Search, social and email each make a promise. People notice when they disagree.

Cognexa team  /  4 min read  /  14 October 2026

In this article

01   Read the journey as one conversation02   Choose one promise to keep03   Test the handover between channels

A customer meets a business in several places. Search may promise the fastest delivery, social the lowest price and email premium quality. When the promises differ, the customer has to decide which one is true.

Read the journey as one conversation

Each channel is a turn in the same conversation. We line up what every channel says about the offer and look for gaps a customer would notice.

A customer remembers the promise and checks it at checkout.

Choose one promise to keep

The strongest message is the one the business can deliver every time. We pick a single lead promise, then shape each channel to support it in its own style.

Channel checks

  • •  What does each channel promise?
  • •  Which promise can we always keep?
  • •  Where do the stories first disagree?

Test the handover between channels

The moment a customer moves from an ad to a page is where mismatches show. We follow real journeys across channels and fix the handover before adding more reach.
