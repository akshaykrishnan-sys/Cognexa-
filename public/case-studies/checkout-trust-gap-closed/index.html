Checkout trust gap closed

A fashion and D2C brand saw shoppers reach checkout and stop. We found the doubt and made the last step feel safe.

01  /  THE BRIEF

Steady traffic, flat checkout

A fashion and D2C brand had steady traffic and a healthy basket, yet checkout completion stayed flat. The team assumed the payment form was too long.

02  /  WHAT WE SAW

Doubt sat at three points

Observation and shopper interviews showed people pausing at the total, the return terms and the payment step. Each pause came from a quiet question about what happens if something goes wrong.

03  /  WHAT WE CHANGED

Three small, tested changes

We showed the total earlier, placed the return terms beside the button and kept the payment step familiar. Each change was tested against one agreed behaviour.

04  /  WHAT MOVED

Completion rose, questions fell

Checkout completion rose by 23% over the test period and support questions about returns fell. The team kept the changes and applied the same method to the product page.
