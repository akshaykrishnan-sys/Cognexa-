06  /  QUESTIONS

What teams ask first

How long does a behavioural insight engagement take for B2B?+

An insight sprint runs 4 to 6 weeks. Design and demand work follow in phases, each aimed at one behaviour in the buying process.

Do you work with long B2B sales cycles?+

Yes. We map every stakeholder in the buying committee and test messages for each role, so the work fits how deals are really decided.

How do you measure impact in B2B?+

Against one agreed behaviour per phase, such as a demo booked or a trial activated. The baseline is agreed before work starts.

Is buyer data kept safe?+

We sign an NDA before any data is shared and work within your security and privacy policies.
