03  /  BEHAVIOURAL INSIGHTS

What patients are really doing

TRUST

Reassurance beats facts

Patients need to feel safe first. Clear, human proof of care settles the first doubt.

CHOICE

Too many options, no action

Long lists of doctors and services stall action. Guide them to one clear next step.

LOSS

Fear of bad news is a cost

People avoid care when the answer might hurt. Frame the visit as gaining control.

HABIT

Waiting feels easier

Delay is the default. Make booking small, quick and the easy thing to do.

06  /  QUESTIONS

What teams ask first

How long does a behavioural insight engagement take in healthcare?+

An insight sprint runs 4 to 6 weeks. Redesign and media work follow in phases, each focused on one step in the patient journey.

Do you follow healthcare regulations?+

Yes. Claims and creative follow medical advertising rules.

How do you measure impact on patient behaviour?+

Against one agreed behaviour per phase, such as a booking made or a follow-up kept. The baseline is agreed before work starts.

Is patient data kept safe?+

We sign an NDA before any data is shared and work within your security and privacy policies.
