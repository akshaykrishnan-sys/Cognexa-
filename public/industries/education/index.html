06  /  QUESTIONS

What teams ask first

How long does a behavioural insight engagement take in education?+

An insight sprint runs 4 to 6 weeks. Redesign and media work follow in phases, each focused on one step from interest to application.

Do you work with university and school admissions cycles?+

Yes. We plan around intakes, deadlines and open days, so changes land when learners and parents are actually deciding.

How do you measure impact on enrolment behaviour?+

Against one agreed behaviour per phase, such as an enquiry made or an application finished. The baseline is agreed before work starts.

Is learner data kept safe?+

We sign an NDA before any data is shared and work within your security and privacy policies.
