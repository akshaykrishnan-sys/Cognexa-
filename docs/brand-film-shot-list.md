# Cognexa brand film: script and shot list (33 seconds)

Theme: our approach and value proposition. No industries.
Voice is recorded and timed. Each shot needs one stock clip, 4 to 8 seconds, landscape, HD or better.
Free sources with commercial licences: Pexels (pexels.com/videos), Mixkit (mixkit.co), Coverr (coverr.co).

| # | Time | Voice-over | On-screen words (in tiles) | Footage to find (search terms) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00 | Every business has a number it wants to move. | CONVERSION · RETENTION · GROWTH | City at dusk, aerial timelapse; dashboard numbers on a screen |
| 2 | 0:04 | But numbers don't make decisions. People do. | PEOPLE DO. | Close-up of a person thinking, looking at a phone, then pausing |
| 3 | 0:07 | So we start with behaviour, not the brief. | BEHAVIOUR FIRST | Hands moving sticky notes on a glass wall; team workshop |

| 6 | 0:14 | and design the change that moves them. | DESIGN THE CHANGE | Designer sketching wireframes; UI on a large monitor |

Treatment (kept from the current film): colour-block tiles with text in every tile, glitch titles, zoom-through and push transitions on the beat, original music, Outfit typeface.
Footage plays inside the tiles and full screen, graded lightly to the brand colours (no heavy duotone).
